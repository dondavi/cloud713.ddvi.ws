Cookies
Cookies (also browser cookies) are small text files. When you browse a website and allow this site to use cookies these files are downloaded on your personal computer. They are stored as identification tags. To provide a smoother experience for your browsing, we handle cookies to enhance some website functionality. With cookies, we can monitor what content you prefer the most; we can deliver information and ads aligned with your tastes and locations; cookies also allow you to easily share information on social media platforms.
